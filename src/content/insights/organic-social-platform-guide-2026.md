Most brands don’t have the people to be great on every platform. The good news: you don’t need to be. The platforms have become more different from each other, which makes choosing easier. This guide summarises what each one rewards, based on recent analysis from [Whitehat SEO](https://whitehat-seo.co.uk/blog/social-media-strategy) and [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/de/blog/instagram-reels-algorithm).

## The big shift: depth over volume

Across platforms, comments are declining while “quiet” signals such as **saves, shares and DMs** carry more weight. Posting more often rarely helps. Posting fewer, better pieces usually does.

## LinkedIn: people, not pages

- Company page posts reportedly reach only 1–2% of the feed.
- Personal profiles reach many times more people with identical content.
- Multi-image posts, PDF carousels and native video lead engagement.
- Posts with external links lose a large share of their reach; put links in the comments or on your profile.

**What to do:** make one or two founders or leaders the voice of the brand. Interview them weekly and turn each interview into posts. Use the company page for hiring and announcements.

## TikTok: a search engine with a feed

TikTok is increasingly where people research, including business buyers. The 35–54 age group is reported as its fastest-growing segment, and professional content is a growing share of what is watched.

**What to do:** answer real questions your customers ask, in short videos with the question in the first line of text and the caption.

## YouTube: Shorts to be found, long-form to be trusted

Channels combining Shorts with long-form reportedly grow around 41% faster. YouTube is also the social platform most cited in Google’s AI Overviews, so a good explainer video can keep working for years.

**What to do:** publish Shorts that link to a longer video on the same topic. Write titles the way people phrase questions.

## Instagram: built for sending

Reels that get sent in DMs and watched to the end reach the most new people. Originality is enforced, and Trial Reels let you test with non-followers. Read our [Reels guide](/insights/instagram-reels-what-the-algorithm-rewards) for the details.

## Facebook and Reddit: underrated

Meta’s targeting has made Facebook useful again, mostly through ads. Reddit threads appear heavily in Google results and AI answers, so taking part genuinely in the right communities builds visibility that compounds.

## How to choose

Pick **one primary platform** where your customers already spend time, and **one supporting platform** that repurposes the same ideas. For most consumer brands that is Instagram plus TikTok or YouTube Shorts. For most B2B brands it is founder-led LinkedIn plus YouTube.
